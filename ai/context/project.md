# Project context: Codeshaper

**What Codeshaper is.** An AI-native software engineering agency. Delivery hub in Dhaka, Bangladesh; collaboration hubs in the US, UK, Japan, Australia and New Zealand; clients in 33 countries. Five service groups (AI Agents & Automation, AI & Software Development, AI Integration & Infrastructure, UI/UX & Product Design, Websites & Commerce), five engagement models (MVP Sprint, End-to-End Delivery, Dedicated Development Team, IT Staff Augmentation, Offshore Development Center) and its own products (Acculance, Dashcode, Banizo).

**Who buys.** Founders and product leaders at startups and growing companies, and technology/operations leaders at established businesses, mostly in North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific. They compare several agencies, read case studies and reviews, and want to know who they will work with and what it costs.

**What the website must do.** Win high-quality software projects: build trust fast, show real proof, make the next step (a call) easy. Secondary: sell the products, attract talent, rank and get cited in search and AI answers.

**Brand feel.** Clean white, minimal, modern, premium, factual. A serious engineering company with taste. Never a template, never an "AI demo".

**Figures in use (owner-supplied, confirm before launch):** 10+ years, 350+ brands, 33 countries (from the globe list), 150+ client reviews. Product facts: Acculance 400+ copies sold (CodeCanyon), Dashcode 2,900+ copies sold (ThemeForest).

**Known gaps (owner to supply):** real client reviews (3 of 4 testimonials are placeholders), review ratings and links, leadership names/photos, Banizo details, real product screenshots, contact details (email, phone, WhatsApp, address), starting prices.
