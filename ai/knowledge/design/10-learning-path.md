# 10 · Learning path

A six-week plan for building design judgement, applied to the Codeshaper website as you go. Each week ends with a real output, not just notes.

| Week | Topic | Practise on | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visual foundations: hierarchy, contrast, alignment, proximity, balance, whitespace ([01](01-visual-principles.md)) | Review 3 homepage sections with the [QA checklist](09-design-qa-checklist.md) | A written critique of each, naming principles |
| 2 | Spacing & composition ([02](02-spacing.md), [05](05-layout.md)) | Redraw one section's layout on a 12-column grid | A layout blueprint (wireframe) |
| 3 | Typography & colour ([03](03-typography.md), [04](04-color.md)) | Audit every heading and paragraph size on one page | A list of off-system values + fixes |
| 4 | Images & visuals ([07](07-imagery.md)) | Replace placeholders with real screens/photos | Hero and product visual direction |
| 5 | Layout & design systems ([06](06-components.md)) | Turn one repeated pattern into a shared component | One new primitive in `src/components/system/` |
| 6 | UX & conversion ([08](08-ux-conversion.md)) | Map the buyer's questions to the homepage | One measured improvement (e.g. booking form completion) |

## Resources (read in this order)

1. **Refactoring UI** (Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger): the most practical visual-design book for developers. <https://www.refactoringui.com>
2. **Laws of UX** (Jon Yablonski): the psychology behind good interfaces. <https://lawsofux.com>
3. **Nielsen Norman Group** articles: usability, forms, trust, research. <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/>
4. **Figma Learn**: components, auto layout, variables, design systems. <https://help.figma.com>
5. **Practical Typography** (Matthew Butterick): type rules that hold everywhere. <https://practicaltypography.com>

## Study method

- For every principle, find **one good and one bad** real website example and write one sentence on why.
- Redesign by **subtraction** first.
- Compare your screenshot with a premium reference side by side at the same width; name the three biggest differences.
